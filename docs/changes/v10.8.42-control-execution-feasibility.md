## Method and constraints
1. Reopen snapshot and verify manifesto, all CSV/action hashes and
   per-asset original binary64 OHLCV numeric hashes before fitting.
2. The original TCC LightGBM fold preparation, labels, calibrations,
   model fitting, temporal purge and scheduled policy are reused intact.
   A **function-local isolated global binding** in the MCT adapter replaces
   only the final scientific OOS accounting simulator. The original
   module's global bindings never change, avoiding a process-wide
   monkeypatch and respecting vendored source integrity.
3. At each prior daily close the scheduled **original Control policy**
   receives the actual constrained position and holding days. This is
   NOT a replay of the baseline's fixed trade list.
4. The following fixed **scenario**, not fitted to outcomes, constrains
   next-session fills: at most 10% of both trailing 20-session median
   volume (at least five finite nonnegative observations; zero-volume days remain in the median) and realized daily
   volume. The prior median is known at the decision; the day's actual
   volume is used **ex post only for the fill constraint**, never by the
   model or at signal selection. Thus the market execution modeled occurs
   over the next session, not as an instantaneous guaranteed open auction.
   Trading at zero-volume sessions is forbidden. The scenario's fill
   price is next open adjusted adversely by half of an **assumed** 15 bps
   full spread plus 20 bps times sqrt(realized volume participation)
   of **assumed** impact. Daily OHLCV has no order book or spread; model
   assumptions cannot prove actual executable fills.
5. Only whole long shares, no shorting, leverage or overlapping assets.
   Partial sells retain the old asset; a new asset is bought **only**
   after old shares are fully disposed of. Partial buys retain unspent
   cash and may top up on following days only if policy still selects
   the held asset. Fees are included in a binary search for affordable
   shares, never producing negative cash. Every daily portfolio is marked
   to close, residual CASH tracked. No unrestricted `FINAL_SELL`;
   ending equity includes stock still held, so may not be liquidatable.
6. The original equal-weight benchmark is included only as the
   **unconstrained benchmark**, not asserted to be itself executable.
   Outcome is a single capital-based execution scenario, not a new
   strategy_mode, not automatic optimization, and **not** a
   live-trading readiness verdict.

### Interpretation and prospective research tests
The bottleneck is concentrated in illiquid VNCE/PXLW fills and
single-position residual holdings, not simply fee schedule. The
$65.7k modeled *contemporaneous* spread/impact is not a causal
decomposition of the $5.36m *compounded* counterfactual delta.

For next **predeclared** research campaign, use the same immutable
source and model protocol; run (A) no capacity cap with otherwise
consistent accounting, (B) capacity cap without extra modeled spread
or impact, (C) capacity cap with the present cost assumptions, and
separate participation sensitivity scenarios (1%, 5%, 10%). Keep
all comparisons, fixed assumptions and chronological folds; do not
select a parameter winner after seeing OOS. For any ex-ante
liquidity-aware candidate selection, create a new conceptual
strategy_mode and restrict features to previous completed sessions.
Do not silently delete VNCE/PXLW solely due to their liquidity
role: structural data-identity exclusions and tradability limits are
distinct research questions. Daily bars cannot prove actual fills.
