## Predeclared mathematical policy
At the COMPLETED decision close t, a candidate i has forecast capacity dollars C_i(t)=0.10*median(volume_i[t-19:t])*close_i(t), with at least 5 finite nonnegative observations. Historical zero-volume sessions count as zero. Actual equity E_t=CASH_t+held_shares_t*known_close_held(t). Entry fraction f_buy_i=min(1,C_i(t)/E_t). If selling held h, exit fraction f_exit_h=min(1,C_h(t)/(held_shares_t*known_close_h(t))). For a new asset, f_i=min(f_buy_i,f_exit_h), or f_i=f_buy_i when in CASH. For incumbent hold f_h=1, because no repurchase is required. Effective candidate utility U_i*=U_i*f_i. CASH utility stays 0. The original Control policy applies its unchanged threshold, margin, minimum-hold and hysteresis logic to U_i*. The policy uses actual portfolio state, not a fixed replay tape. All 55 scores are audited on each OOS date. This is a fixed expected deployable-utility hypothesis, not a fitted liquidity predictor or guaranteed fill model.

The liquidity forecast uses only prior completed-session volume and close. Its inputs never use execution-day market data. CAUTION: original frozen TCC utility precomputation has an inherited next-session open/close availability check for candidate score validity; original source is unchanged. The new liquidity transform itself is prior-close causal, but end-to-end model causality needs a separate availability audit before any operational claim.
