## Interpretation
Comparison of `no_cap_no_cost` and `cap10_no_cost` describes
the **total** pathway difference caused by modeled capacity with
otherwise consistent accounting. Comparison `cap10_no_cost` vs
`cap10_cost` describes the total pathway difference when the
assumed price costs are added. These are not additive causal
attributions: positions and future decisions are state-dependent,
so the interaction matters. Report all five cases without
ranking, promotion or post-hoc OOS optimization. The 1%, 5% and
10% scenarios are sensitivity assumptions, not parameters tuned
from the test period. Liquidity-aware candidate screening would
require a separate conceptual strategy_mode and features restricted
to completed prior-session data. Do not exclude VNCE/PXLW as if
illiquidity were a structural identity defect.
