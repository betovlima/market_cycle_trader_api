All cases retain the original brokerage reference fee helper,
whole long shares, no leverage/overlap, CASH leftovers, original
price and next-session close marks, and no hypothetical final sale.
The no-cap comparator **may purchase above 100% daily volume or in
a zero-volume session by design**; it is not executable. It is also
not accounting-identical to the old frozen scientific TCC replay
(which used fractional holdings and a final bookkeeping sell).
No-cap output must never be mislabeled as an independently verified
$5.887m reproduction.

The capacity-constrained scenarios use realized same-day volume
**ex post to determine fills only**; never to select or size the
previous-close signal. Historical zero-volume days are retained in
the prior 20-session median. Fill prices are the next open plus
assumed adverse half-spread and square-root price impact, not observed
auction/order-book fills. A partially sold old asset blocks entering
a new one; subsequent Control decisions respond to actual holdings.

The `cap10_cost` result must pass fail-closed numerical parity
against the completed v10.8.42 metrics (capital, CAGR, Sharpe,
drawdown, fills, rotations, CASH, price cost and ending asset) before

## Interpretation
Comparison of `no_cap_no_cost` and `cap10_no_cost` describes
the **total** pathway difference caused by modeled capacity with
otherwise consistent accounting. Comparison `cap10_no_cost` vs
`cap10_cost` describes the total pathway difference when the
assumed price costs are added. These are not additive causal
attributions: positions and future decisions are state-dependent,
so the interaction matters. Report all five cases without
ranking, promotion or post-hoc OOS optimization. The 1%, 5% and
10% scenarios are sensitivity assumptions, not parameters tuned
from the test period. Liquidity-aware candidate screening would
require a separate conceptual strategy_mode and features restricted
to completed prior-session data. Do not exclude VNCE/PXLW as if
illiquidity were a structural identity defect.
