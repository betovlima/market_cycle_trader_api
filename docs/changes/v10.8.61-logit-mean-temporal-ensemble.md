## Logit-mean aggregation

Instead of averaging probabilities directly, combine log-odds:

`P = sigmoid(mean(logit(P_component)))`

This is equivalent to taking the geometric mean of component odds.

No weight, exponent or fitted ensemble coefficient is introduced.

The veto rule remains:

`P <= 0.35`
