## Purpose

This is a deliberately small proof-of-structure experiment. It does **not** try to search the whole market and it does not assume that a profitable asset in the past will remain profitable in the future.

The question is narrower:

> Does information available at decision time `t` contain a stable mathematical signal about the exact future marginal contribution of candidate `a` to the current universe `U`?

For each decision date and candidate, the label is generated by two exact Strategy replays over the same future analysis window:

`Delta(a | U, t, H) = log C(U + a, t:t+H) - log C(U, t:t+H)`

The exact capital judge, model fitting, policy, fees and slippage are unchanged. `analysis_start_date` and `analysis_end_date` constrain the executable out-of-sample interval while the walk-forward engine retains historical data before `t` for fitting/calibration.

## Point-in-time feature policy

Inputs are restricted to backward-looking Strategy features known at `t` plus relative/context variables. Forward target columns from `build_rotation_frame` are never used as model inputs.

The first compact model uses:

- candidate minus current-universe mean for return 5/20/60, volatility 20/60, EMA distance 20, trend efficiency 20 and momentum acceleration 5/20;
- candidate correlation to equal-weight universe returns over 20 and 60 sessions;
- cross-sectional universe dispersion;
- candidate percentile rank for return 20 and return 60;
- SPY 20-session return/volatility as a simple regime proxy when SPY is present.

The aim is not to claim these variables are the final signature. They are a small interpretable basis for testing whether any stable structure exists before a larger causal experiment is justified.

## Recommended microexperiment

The recommended run uses the manually chosen Original25 as the fixed base universe and 12 candidates that showed materially different historical marginal behavior in the exact-search experiments:

`GKOS DNN CORT MKSI VNCE UNFI APD YANG CCK CXW CLMT XSD`

It samples 20 deterministic monthly decision dates between 2019 and 2025 and measures the next 40 XNYS sessions. That creates a target panel of approximately **240 exact candidate/date labels**, plus 20 shared baseline replays.

The temporal validation split is fixed at 2024-01-01: relationships are fitted only on earlier dates and evaluated on later dates.

## What counts as evidence

A strong in-sample correlation is **not** enough.

The first experiment is useful only if relationships discovered before the validation boundary retain useful information after the boundary. Useful evidence includes:

- positive out-of-time correlation between predicted and realized marginal log-capital;
- positive cross-sectional rank correlation within future decision dates;
- candidate selected by the simple model beating the average candidate often enough to be economically meaningful;
- feature effects whose direction is reasonably stable between training and future validation.

A weak or unstable result is also informative: it tells us not to spend weeks generating a much larger counterfactual dataset with the same feature representation.

## Scientific limits

This experiment remains historical research. The decision dates are sampled from a period that has already been observed in previous Market Cycle Trader research, so it is not untouched final OOS evidence. The purpose is only to test whether a contextual mathematical signature appears to exist and whether it survives a chronological holdout inside the historical laboratory.

If the microexperiment shows stable structure, the next step can expand the panel and evaluate causal-forest / DML-style heterogeneous effects. If it does not, we should revise the representation of `asset × universe × regime` before spending more compute.
